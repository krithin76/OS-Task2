Working Principle

1. A reader executes wait(mutex) before changing readerCount.

2. The first reader executes wait(wrt), preventing writers from entering.

3. Multiple readers can read the database simultaneously.

4. The last reader executes signal(wrt), allowing a writer to enter.

5. A writer executes wait(wrt) before writing and signal(wrt) afterward.

Note: This simulation demonstrates the readers-preference solution. Continuous arrivals of readers may cause writer starvation.
